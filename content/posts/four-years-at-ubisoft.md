Four years of my career were at Ubisoft, on Assassin's Creed, as a gameplay programmer. Most of my work was data-driven gameplay code and internal ImGui debugging tools: things designers use to control how the gameplay behaves, without needing a programmer for every change.

Before Assassin's Creed Shadows launched, I spent about four months on bug fixing, from its original release date in November 2024 until it shipped in March 2025: cinematics, the health bar, combat mechanics and AI.

My longest task there was migrating a legacy gameplay system I inherited half-finished, toward a modular, data-driven design. However, the task stalled for months, waiting on information that never came, and I eventually handed it over unfinished.

At some point the team split: core gameplay systems moved to Ubisoft Quebec and Montreal, the studios that own Assassin's Creed, while gameplay features (character, camera and controls, combat AI, puzzles) stayed a collaboration with Ubisoft Romania. The kind of restructuring the games industry keeps going through, not something specific to me. In the middle of that transition I worked briefly on an unreleased Assassin's Creed project, where I delivered Pressure Plates, a first working prototype of the mechanic, in about a month.

That month stuck with me more than I expected. Not because it was easy, but because I noticed something about how I work best: under a clear external deadline, with a concrete result at the end, I produce. It's not the first time I've noticed this pattern. Cindy's Adventures, my solo entry for the Game Off 2021 jam: built entirely in a month, with the jam's rules as the only deadline, and it shipped finished. Same with EvoAgents, my bachelor's thesis on behavior trees for NPC AI: a fixed academic deadline, a clear deliverable, seen through to the end. My projects without an external deadline? Most of them stayed good ideas on paper at that time.

I don't have a clean conclusion about where my career goes, and I don't think I need one right now. What I do have is a clear pattern for how I work best and things that genuinely interest me.
